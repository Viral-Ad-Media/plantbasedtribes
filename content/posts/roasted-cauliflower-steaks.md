Cauliflower steaks are an elegant way to turn a humble vegetable into a main course. The relish of golden raisins, capers, vinegar and herbs is sweet, salty and tangy all at once, and makes a perfect contrast to the nutty roasted cauliflower.

## Ingredients

- 2 large heads cauliflower
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper

**Raisin relish**

- ⅓ cup (50 g) golden raisins
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
- 1 small shallot, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- Large handful of parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp toasted pine nuts

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Trim the leaves and cut each cauliflower through the stem into two 2.5 cm thick steaks. Save the loose florets for another recipe.
2. Brush both sides with oil, sprinkle with paprika and season.
3. Sear in a hot ovenproof pan for 3 minutes per side until golden, then transfer to the oven for 15–20 minutes until tender.
4. Meanwhile, soak the raisins in the vinegar for 10 minutes. Stir in the capers, shallot, oil, parsley and pine nuts.
5. Serve the steaks topped with a generous spoonful of relish.

## Tips

- Roast the leftover florets alongside the steaks to serve as a side.
