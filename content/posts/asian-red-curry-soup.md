This soup is all about the broth: red curry paste, coconut milk, lime and a splash of soy sauce combine into something rich, spicy and fragrant. Load it up with whatever vegetables you have and some golden tofu for a complete meal.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 2–3 tbsp vegan Thai red curry paste
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 × 400 ml tin coconut milk
- 750 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 handfuls of mushrooms, sliced
- 2 pak choi, quartered
- 150 g rice noodles
- Juice of 1 lime
- Coriander, Thai basil and sliced chilli, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Fry the curry paste, ginger and garlic for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
2. Pour in the coconut milk, stock, soy sauce and sugar. Bring to a simmer.
3. Add the tofu, pepper and mushrooms and simmer for 8 minutes.
4. Add the pak choi for the final 2 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, cook the noodles according to the packet and divide between bowls.
6. Stir the lime juice into the broth and ladle it over the noodles. Top with herbs and chilli.

## Tips

- Many shop-bought curry pastes contain shrimp paste or fish sauce. Check the label.
- Use tamari to keep it gluten-free.
