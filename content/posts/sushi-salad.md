Love sushi but don't have time to roll it? This deconstructed sushi bowl has all the same flavours: seasoned sushi rice topped with avocado, cucumber, carrot, edamame and pickled ginger, finished with crumbled nori and spicy vegan mayo.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups (300 g) sushi rice
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 cup (150 g) shelled edamame
- 2 nori sheets, cut into strips
- Pickled ginger, sesame seeds and tamari, to serve

**Spicy mayo**

- 4 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
- 1–2 tsp sriracha
- 1 tsp lime juice

## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook according to the packet.
2. Warm the vinegar, sugar and salt until dissolved, then fold into the hot rice. Let cool slightly.
3. Mix the spicy mayo ingredients.
4. Divide the rice between bowls and arrange the vegetables on top.
5. Drizzle with spicy mayo and finish with nori, pickled ginger and sesame seeds. Serve with tamari.

## Tips

- Add cubes of marinated tofu for extra protein.
