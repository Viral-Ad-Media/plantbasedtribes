This bright golden soup is what we make when we need something warming and nourishing. Turmeric and fresh ginger give it a gentle heat, sweet potato makes it naturally creamy, and a handful of red lentils adds protein.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coconut or olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger, grated
- 2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and cubed
- 2 carrots, chopped
- ½ cup (100 g) red lentils, rinsed
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 × 400 ml tin light coconut milk
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt, black pepper and fresh coriander

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot and cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
2. Add the garlic, ginger, turmeric and cumin and stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the sweet potato, carrot, lentils and stock. Simmer for 20–25 minutes until everything is very soft.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and blend until smooth.
5. Add lime juice, season with salt and a good grind of black pepper, and top with coriander.

## Tips

- Black pepper helps your body absorb the curcumin in turmeric, so don't skip it.
- Stir through a handful of spinach for extra greens.
