Dried black beans are incredibly cheap and full of flavour, and the pressure cooker turns them silky-tender in under an hour without any soaking. Cumin, oregano and chipotle give this soup a gentle smoky warmth, and a squeeze of lime at the end makes it sing.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, chopped (or 1 tsp smoked paprika)
- 2 cups (400 g) dried black beans, rinsed
- 1.5 litres vegetable stock
- 1 bay leaf
- Juice of 1 lime
- Salt to taste

**To serve:** avocado, coriander, plant yoghurt, tortilla chips

## Instructions

1. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté. Heat the oil and cook the onion, pepper and carrot for 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, cumin, oregano and chipotle and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the beans, stock and bay leaf. Stir and scrape the bottom of the pot so nothing sticks.
4. Seal and cook on High Pressure for 40 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 15 minutes.
5. Remove the bay leaf. Blend about a third of the soup and stir it back in.
6. Add the lime juice and season with salt. Serve with your favourite toppings.

## Tips

- Don't salt until the end; it's easier to get the balance right.
- No pressure cooker? Use 3 tins of black beans and simmer on the stove for 25 minutes.
