Cabbage soup has a reputation as diet food, but made properly it's genuinely tasty. The trick is to build flavour first with softened onion, garlic and a little smoked paprika, then let the cabbage simmer just until tender so it keeps a little sweetness and bite.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery sticks, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ green or savoy cabbage, shredded (about 500 g)
- 1 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1.5 litres vegetable stock
- Salt, pepper and chopped parsley

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Cook the onion, carrot and celery for 8 minutes until softened.
2. Add the garlic and paprika and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the cabbage, tomatoes and stock. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 20 minutes until the cabbage is tender.
4. Season well and finish with chopped parsley.

## Tips

- Add a tin of white beans or a handful of red lentils to make it more filling.
- A squeeze of lemon or a splash of apple cider vinegar at the end lifts all the flavours.
