Roasting cauliflower at a high temperature transforms it: the edges turn nutty and golden while the inside stays tender. Here it's tossed with chickpeas, herbs and jewel-like pomegranate seeds and finished with a creamy tahini sauce.

## Ingredients

- 1 large cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- Large handful each of parsley and mint
- Seeds from ½ pomegranate
- 2 tbsp toasted almonds, chopped

**Tahini sauce**

- 3 tbsp tahini
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 small garlic clove, grated
- 3–4 tbsp cold water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Toss the cauliflower and chickpeas with the oil, spices and salt. Spread on a large tray.
2. Roast for 25 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.
3. Whisk the tahini, lemon, garlic and salt. Add water a spoonful at a time until smooth and pourable.
4. Toss the warm cauliflower with the herbs. Transfer to a platter, drizzle with tahini sauce and scatter with pomegranate and almonds.

## Tips

- Tahini seizes up when you first add lemon. Keep whisking and adding water and it will turn silky.
