If you've ever had the sweet, zingy carrot dressing served with salads in Japanese restaurants, you'll know how addictive it is. Here it's paired with massaged kale, creamy avocado and protein-rich edamame for a salad that's both refreshing and filling.

## Ingredients

- 1 large bunch of kale, stems removed, leaves chopped
- 1 tsp olive oil and a pinch of salt (for massaging)
- 1 cup (150 g) shelled edamame, defrosted
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

**Carrot ginger dressing**

- 2 medium carrots, roughly chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, chopped
- 1 small shallot
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp tamari
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 3 tbsp neutral oil
- 2–3 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Blend all the dressing ingredients until smooth and slightly chunky. Add more water if it's too thick.
2. Put the kale in a large bowl with the oil and salt. Massage with your hands for 2–3 minutes until it darkens and softens.
3. Add the edamame and cucumber and toss with half the dressing.
4. Top with the avocado and sesame seeds, and serve the remaining dressing on the side.

## Tips

- The dressing keeps for 5 days in the fridge and is great on rice bowls.
- Massaged kale holds up well, so this salad is ideal for packed lunches (add the avocado just before eating).
