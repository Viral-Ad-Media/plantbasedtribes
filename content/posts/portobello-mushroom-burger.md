Big, juicy portobello mushrooms make a fantastic burger. A quick balsamic and garlic marinade gives them deep, savoury flavour, and a few minutes on a hot grill gives them a smoky char. Stack them with caramelised onions and a garlicky vegan aioli.

## Ingredients

- 4 large portobello mushrooms, stems removed
- 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 4 burger buns, toasted
- Lettuce, tomato and rocket

**Garlic aioli**

- 4 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
- 1 small garlic clove, grated
- 1 tsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Whisk the balsamic, oil, soy sauce, garlic and thyme. Pour over the mushrooms and marinate for 10–15 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, cook the onion in a little oil over medium-low heat for 15 minutes until soft and caramelised.
3. Mix the aioli ingredients.
4. Grill or griddle the mushrooms over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes per side until tender, brushing with leftover marinade.
5. Spread the buns with aioli and layer with lettuce, tomato, mushroom and onions.

## Tips

- Add a slice of vegan cheese to the mushroom for the last minute of cooking.
