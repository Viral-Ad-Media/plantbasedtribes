This smoothie tastes like a slice of pumpkin pie but is made from wholesome ingredients. Pumpkin purée and frozen banana make it thick and creamy, oats add staying power and a pinch of pumpkin spice makes it taste like autumn.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup (120 g) pumpkin purée
- 1 frozen banana
- 2 tbsp rolled oats
- 1 tbsp almond butter
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice (or cinnamon with a pinch of nutmeg and ginger)
- ½ tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup (240 ml) almond or oat milk
- Handful of ice

## Instructions

1. Add everything to a blender.
2. Blend for about 60 seconds until thick and creamy.
3. Pour into glasses and dust with a little cinnamon.

## Tips

- Freeze leftover pumpkin purée in ice cube trays so it's ready for smoothies.
