Making sushi at home is fun, especially with friends or kids. You don't need any fancy equipment beyond a bamboo mat, and vegetable fillings are cheap and colourful. Once you've got the hang of rolling, you'll be making these every week.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (400 g) sushi rice
- 2½ cups (600 ml) water
- 4 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1½ tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 nori sheets
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 cucumber, cut into long thin strips
- 1 carrot, cut into long thin strips
- 1 red pepper, cut into thin strips
- Tamari, pickled ginger and wasabi to serve

## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear. Cook with the water in a covered pan for 12 minutes, then rest off the heat for 10 minutes.
2. Dissolve the sugar and salt in the vinegar and fold gently through the rice. Cover with a damp towel and let cool to room temperature.
3. Place a nori sheet shiny-side down on a bamboo mat. With wet hands, spread a thin layer of rice over it, leaving a 2 cm strip at the top.
4. Lay a few strips of each vegetable along the bottom edge.
5. Roll tightly away from you using the mat, pressing gently. Dampen the top edge to seal.
6. Slice each roll into 6–8 pieces with a sharp, wet knife. Serve with tamari, ginger and wasabi.

## Tips

- Keep a bowl of water nearby. Wet hands stop the rice sticking.
- Try other fillings: mango, sweet potato tempura, marinated tofu or pickled radish.
