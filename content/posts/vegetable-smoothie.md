If you're new to vegetable smoothies, start here. Apple and banana add natural sweetness, ginger and lemon keep it fresh, and you'll barely notice the spinach, cucumber and celery.

## Ingredients

- 2 large handfuls of spinach
- ½ cucumber, chopped
- 1 celery stick, chopped
- 1 green apple, cored and chopped
- 1 frozen banana
- 1 cm piece of fresh ginger
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 cup (240 ml) cold water or coconut water
- Handful of ice

## Instructions

1. Add the liquid to the blender first, followed by the spinach, then the rest of the ingredients.
2. Blend on high for 60 seconds until completely smooth.
3. Taste and add a little more apple or a date if you'd like it sweeter. Serve straight away.

## Tips

- Add a tablespoon of hemp seeds or a scoop of plant protein to make it more filling.
