Smashing cucumbers rather than slicing them creates rough edges that soak up dressing beautifully. Paired with golden pan-fried tofu and a spicy peanut sauce, it's a salad that easily makes a full meal.

## Ingredients

- 200 g extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 1 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 cucumbers
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- Handful of coriander
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds

**Spicy peanut dressing**

- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 1–2 tsp chilli oil or sriracha
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 2–3 tbsp warm water

## Instructions

1. Toss the tofu in cornflour and a pinch of salt. Fry in the oil over medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes, turning, until golden and crisp.
2. Lay the cucumbers on a board and smash lightly with the flat of a knife. Cut into bite-sized chunks, sprinkle with a pinch of salt and leave for 5 minutes, then drain.
3. Whisk the dressing ingredients together until smooth.
4. Toss the cucumber with half the dressing. Top with the tofu, drizzle with the rest and scatter with spring onions, coriander and sesame seeds.

## Tips

- Use tamari and gluten-free cornflour for a gluten-free version.
- Serve over rice or noodles to make it more substantial.
