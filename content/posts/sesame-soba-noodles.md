Soba noodles have a lovely nutty flavour that pairs perfectly with a sesame dressing. This dish is ready in under 20 minutes, can be served chilled or warm, and makes an excellent packed lunch.

## Ingredients

- 250 g soba noodles
- 1 cup (150 g) shelled edamame
- 1 cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- Handful of coriander

**Sesame dressing**

- 3 tbsp tahini or Chinese sesame paste
- 3 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2–3 tbsp warm water

## Instructions

1. Cook the soba according to the packet, adding the edamame for the last 2 minutes. Drain and rinse well under cold water to remove the starch.
2. Whisk the dressing ingredients until smooth.
3. Toss the noodles and edamame with the dressing, cucumber, carrot and most of the spring onions.
4. Serve topped with sesame seeds, coriander and the remaining spring onions.

## Tips

- For gluten-free, use 100% buckwheat soba (many brands contain wheat) and tamari.
