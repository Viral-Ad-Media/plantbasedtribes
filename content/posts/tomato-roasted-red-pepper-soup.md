Roasting tomatoes and red peppers together concentrates their sweetness and adds a lovely smoky edge. Everything goes onto one tray, so the oven does most of the work. Blend, season and you've got a soup that tastes far more complicated than it is.

## Ingredients

- 6 ripe tomatoes, halved
- 3 red peppers, seeded and quartered
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 750 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- Salt, pepper and fresh basil

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Spread the tomatoes, peppers, onion and garlic on a large tray. Drizzle with oil, sprinkle with paprika and season.
2. Roast for 35–40 minutes until soft and charred in places.
3. Squeeze the garlic from its skins and transfer everything to a pot with the stock. Bring to a simmer.
4. Blend until smooth, then stir in the balsamic vinegar and season to taste.
5. Serve with basil and crusty bread.

## Tips

- Short on time? Use a jar of roasted red peppers and a tin of tomatoes, simmered for 15 minutes.
- Stir in a few tablespoons of oat cream for an extra-creamy finish.
