Once you've tasted homemade almond milk, the cartons never quite compare. It's creamy and fresh-tasting, contains nothing but what you put in it and takes about 10 minutes of hands-on time. Save the leftover pulp for baking.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (140 g) raw almonds
- 4 cups (1 litre) filtered water, plus extra for soaking
- 1–2 dates or 1 tbsp maple syrup (optional)
- ½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Cover the almonds with water and soak for 8–12 hours or overnight. Drain and rinse.
2. Blend the almonds with the fresh water, dates, vanilla and salt on high for 1–2 minutes until very smooth.
3. Pour through a nut milk bag or a sieve lined with muslin. Squeeze firmly to extract all the milk.
4. Transfer to a clean bottle and refrigerate.

## Tips

- Homemade almond milk keeps for 3–4 days in the fridge. Shake before using, as it separates naturally.
- Use less water for a richer milk you can use like cream in coffee.
- Spread the leftover pulp on a tray and dry it in a low oven to make almond flour.
