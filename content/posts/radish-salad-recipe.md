Radishes are often overlooked, but thinly sliced they make a beautifully crisp, peppery salad. With cucumber, spring onions and a bright lemon-dill dressing, this is the perfect side for barbecues and spring lunches.

## Ingredients

- 2 bunches radishes (about 300 g), thinly sliced
- 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- Small bunch of dill, chopped
- Handful of parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Combine the radishes, cucumber, spring onions and herbs in a bowl.
2. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice and mustard with a good pinch of salt and pepper.
3. Pour the dressing over the vegetables, toss and serve straight away.

## Tips

- Use a mandoline for very thin, even slices.
- Add a spoonful of plant yoghurt to the dressing for a creamy version.
