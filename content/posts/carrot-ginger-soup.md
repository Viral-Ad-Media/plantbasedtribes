Carrot and ginger are a classic pairing for good reason. The natural sweetness of the carrots balances the warm heat of fresh ginger, and a splash of coconut milk makes it smooth and luscious. It's a lovely starter or a light lunch with some bread.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive or coconut oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 800 g carrots, chopped
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 200 ml coconut milk
- Juice of ½ orange (optional)
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a pot and cook the onion for 5 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the carrots and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the carrots are very soft.
4. Add the coconut milk and orange juice and blend until smooth.
5. Season and serve with a swirl of coconut milk and a few chilli flakes.

## Tips

- The more ginger, the more warming the soup. Adjust to taste.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months.
