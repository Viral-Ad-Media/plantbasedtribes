Everything you love about tacos, in soup form. The broth gets its smoky depth from chipotle and cumin, black beans and sweetcorn make it hearty, and the toppings are where the fun is: crispy baked tortilla strips, creamy avocado, lime and coriander.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chipotle powder or smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 × 400 g tin black beans, drained
- 1 cup (150 g) sweetcorn
- Juice of 1 lime
- 4 corn tortillas, cut into strips

**To serve:** avocado, coriander, sliced jalapeño, lime wedges

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the tortilla strips with a little oil and salt and bake for 8–10 minutes until crisp.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large pot. Cook the onion and pepper for 6 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, cumin, chipotle and oregano and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the tomatoes, stock, beans and corn. Simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Stir in the lime juice and season.
6. Ladle into bowls and pile on tortilla strips, avocado, coriander and jalapeño.

## Tips

- Use certified gluten-free corn tortillas if you're cooking for someone with coeliac disease.
- Add the tortilla strips just before eating so they stay crunchy.
