This show-stopping tart looks like it came from a French patisserie but is surprisingly simple. A press-in crust of almonds and oats, a creamy vanilla filling and a colourful topping of whatever fruit is at its best.

## Ingredients

**Crust**

- 1 cup (100 g) ground almonds
- 1 cup (90 g) gluten-free rolled oats, blended into flour
- 3 tbsp coconut oil, melted
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- Pinch of salt

**Filling**

- 1 × 400 ml tin full-fat coconut milk
- 3 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract or paste

**Topping**

- About 400 g mixed fresh fruit: strawberries, raspberries, blueberries, kiwi, peaches
- 1 tbsp apricot jam, warmed (optional, for glazing)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Mix the crust ingredients and press firmly into a 23 cm tart tin. Bake for 12–15 minutes until golden. Cool.
2. Whisk the coconut milk, cornflour and maple syrup in a pan. Cook over medium heat, whisking, until thick, about 5 minutes. Stir in the vanilla.
3. Pour the filling into the cooled crust and chill for at least 2 hours until set.
4. Arrange the fruit on top and brush with warmed jam for a glossy finish.

## Tips

- Assemble the fruit no more than a few hours before serving.
