Good tofu is all about the marinade. This one is salty, tangy and slightly sweet, and baking the tofu afterwards gives it a satisfyingly chewy texture. Served over crunchy cabbage, carrots and herbs with the extra marinade as dressing, it's a bright and filling salad.

## Ingredients

- 400 g extra-firm tofu, pressed
- ¼ red cabbage, finely shredded
- 2 carrots, julienned
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 1 cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- Handful each of coriander and mint
- 2 tbsp roasted peanuts or cashews

**Marinade and dressing**

- 3 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- Juice of 1 lime

## Instructions

1. Whisk the marinade ingredients together. Cut the tofu into thin slices or cubes and toss with half the marinade. Leave for at least 15 minutes (or overnight).
2. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Spread the tofu on a lined tray and bake for 25 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.
3. Toss the vegetables and herbs together with the remaining marinade.
4. Top with the warm tofu and nuts.

## Tips

- Freezing and defrosting tofu before marinating makes it extra chewy and absorbent.
