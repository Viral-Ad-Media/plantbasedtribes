These muffins rise tall thanks to a hot oven and a "buttermilk" made from plant milk and lemon juice. They're soft and tender, packed with juicy blueberries and finished with a sprinkle of sugar for a crunchy top.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (240 ml) plant milk
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) neutral oil
- ¾ cup (150 g) caster sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 2 cups (250 g) plain flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1½ cups (225 g) blueberries, fresh or frozen
- 2 tbsp demerara sugar, for topping

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a 12-hole muffin tin.
2. Stir the lemon juice into the milk and leave for 5 minutes to curdle slightly.
3. Whisk in the oil, sugar, vanilla and lemon zest.
4. In another bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt. Toss the blueberries in 1 tbsp of the flour mixture.
5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and fold until just combined. A few lumps are fine. Fold in the blueberries.
6. Divide between the cases, sprinkle with demerara sugar and bake for 20–22 minutes.

## Tips

- Don't overmix the batter or the muffins will be tough.
- If using frozen blueberries, add them straight from the freezer.
