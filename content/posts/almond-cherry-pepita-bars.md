These are our favourite on-the-go snack. Toasted oats, almonds and pepitas are bound with almond butter and maple syrup, studded with tart dried cherries, then pressed into a tin and chilled. No oven needed.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (180 g) gluten-free rolled oats
- ½ cup (70 g) almonds, chopped
- ⅓ cup (45 g) pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
- ½ cup (75 g) dried cherries, chopped
- ½ cup (125 g) almond butter
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) maple syrup or brown rice syrup
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Toast the oats, almonds and pumpkin seeds in a dry pan over medium heat for 5 minutes, stirring often. Tip into a bowl with the cherries.
2. Gently warm the almond butter, maple syrup and coconut oil until smooth. Stir in the vanilla and salt.
3. Pour over the dry ingredients and mix until everything is coated.
4. Press very firmly into a lined 20 cm square tin.
5. Chill for at least 1 hour, then cut into 12 bars.

## Tips

- Keep in the fridge for up to a week, or freeze for a month.
- Swap cherries for cranberries or chopped apricots.
