A chopped salad means every forkful has a bit of everything. Here that's crunchy romaine, marinated artichokes, olives, roasted peppers, chickpeas and sun-dried tomatoes. A simple red-wine-vinegar dressing with oregano ties it all together.

## Ingredients

- 1 large romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 1 jar (280 g) marinated artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
- ½ cup (75 g) roasted red peppers, chopped
- ⅓ cup (50 g) Kalamata olives, halved
- ¼ cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- ½ red onion, finely diced
- Pepperoncini, to taste

**Italian dressing**

- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Shake the dressing ingredients together in a jar.
2. Combine all the salad ingredients in a large bowl.
3. Pour over the dressing, toss well and serve straight away.

## Tips

- Add cubes of smoked tofu or vegan mozzarella for extra protein.
- Prep the toppings ahead and keep them separate from the lettuce until serving.
