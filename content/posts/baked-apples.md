Baked apples taste like apple crumble with half the effort. The cored apples are filled with a buttery oat and walnut mixture and baked until the fruit is soft and the filling is golden. Serve warm with vegan custard or ice cream.

## Ingredients

- 4 firm baking apples (such as Braeburn or Honeycrisp)
- ⅓ cup (30 g) rolled oats
- ¼ cup (30 g) walnuts, chopped
- 2 tbsp raisins
- 2 tbsp brown sugar or maple syrup
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 2 tbsp vegan butter or coconut oil
- ½ cup (120 ml) apple juice or water

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Core the apples, leaving the base intact so the filling doesn't fall out. Score a line around the middle of each apple to stop it bursting.
3. Mix the oats, walnuts, raisins, sugar, cinnamon and butter.
4. Stuff the apples and place them in a small baking dish. Pour the apple juice around them.
5. Bake for 35–45 minutes, basting once, until the apples are tender.
6. Serve warm with the syrupy juices spooned over.

## Tips

- Use certified gluten-free oats for a gluten-free dessert.
