When the barbecue is lit, make a big platter of this. Smoky, charred vegetables taste wonderful with a sharp lemon and garlic dressing and plenty of fresh herbs. Serve it warm or at room temperature as a side or a main with crusty bread.

## Ingredients

- 2 courgettes (zucchini), sliced lengthways
- 1 aubergine (eggplant), sliced into rounds
- 2 peppers, cut into wide strips
- 2 corn cobs
- 1 red onion, cut into thick rounds
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Handful of basil and mint leaves

**Dressing**

- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- Juice and zest of 1 lemon
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat a barbecue or griddle pan until very hot.
2. Brush the vegetables with olive oil and season with salt.
3. Grill in batches, turning once, until tender and charred: about 3–4 minutes per side for most vegetables and 10–12 minutes for the corn, turning often.
4. Whisk the dressing ingredients together.
5. Cut the corn from the cob and arrange all the vegetables on a platter. Pour over the dressing and scatter with herbs.

## Tips

- Add a tin of white beans or some grilled tofu to make it a full meal.
- Leftovers are fantastic in wraps.
