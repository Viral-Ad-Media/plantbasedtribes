Inspired by the legendary Italian cookbook author Marcella Hazan, this soup proves how little you need for something delicious. Garlic gently sizzled in olive oil, a generous handful of parsley and creamy cannellini beans. Part of the soup is mashed to thicken it, so every spoonful is rich and comforting.

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus extra to serve
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 2 × 400 g tins cannellini beans, with their liquid
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- Salt and black pepper
- Toasted bread, to serve

## Instructions

1. Warm the oil and garlic together in a pot over medium-low heat. Cook gently until the garlic turns pale gold, about 2 minutes. Don't let it brown.
2. Add the parsley and stir for a few seconds.
3. Add the beans with their liquid and stir to coat. Cook for 5 minutes.
4. Scoop out about a cup of beans, mash them to a paste and return them to the pot.
5. Add the stock and simmer for 10 minutes. Season well.
6. Place a slice of toasted bread in each bowl, ladle the soup over and finish with a drizzle of olive oil.

## Tips

- Use gluten-free bread if needed. The soup itself is naturally gluten-free.
- A squeeze of lemon and a pinch of chilli flakes are lovely additions.
