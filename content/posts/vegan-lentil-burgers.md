The biggest complaint about veggie burgers is that they fall apart. These don't. Lentils, oats and walnuts give them a firm, meaty texture, and smoked paprika and soy sauce add plenty of savoury flavour.

## Ingredients

- 1 × 400 g tin brown lentils, drained (or 1½ cups cooked)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 carrot, grated
- ¾ cup (70 g) rolled oats
- ½ cup (50 g) walnuts
- 1 tbsp ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tbsp water
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- Salt and pepper
- 6 buns and your favourite burger toppings

## Instructions

1. Cook the onion in the oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and carrot and cook for 3 minutes more.
2. Pulse the oats and walnuts in a food processor until coarse. Add the lentils, cooked vegetables, flax mixture, soy sauce, tomato paste and spices. Pulse until it holds together but still has texture.
3. Shape into 6 patties and chill for 20 minutes to firm up.
4. Fry in a little oil over medium heat for 4–5 minutes per side, or bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20 minutes, turning once.
5. Serve in buns with lettuce, tomato, pickles and sauce.

## Tips

- Freeze uncooked patties between sheets of baking paper for quick dinners.
