Red lentils are a weeknight hero. They need no soaking, cook in about 20 minutes and melt into a thick, satisfying soup. This version is gently spiced with cumin and turmeric and brightened with plenty of lemon at the end. It costs very little to make and keeps you full for hours.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 cup (200 g) red lentils, rinsed
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock
- 2 large handfuls of spinach
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion and carrot for 6–7 minutes until softened.
2. Stir in the garlic, cumin and turmeric and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the lentils and stock. Bring to the boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils have broken down.
4. Blend about half the soup for a creamy texture with some bite, or leave it chunky.
5. Stir in the spinach until wilted, then add the lemon juice. Season well.

## Tips

- Lentil soup thickens as it sits. Loosen with a splash of water when reheating.
- Top with a spoonful of plant yoghurt and a pinch of chilli flakes.
- Each serving provides roughly 13 g of plant protein.
