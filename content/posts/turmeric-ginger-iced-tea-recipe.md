This sunny iced tea is a refreshing alternative to sugary soft drinks. Fresh turmeric and ginger are simmered to make a warming brew, then sweetened lightly and chilled over ice with plenty of lemon.

## Ingredients

- 1 litre water
- 5 cm piece fresh turmeric, sliced (or 1 tsp ground turmeric)
- 5 cm piece fresh ginger, sliced
- 1 cinnamon stick
- A few black peppercorns
- 2–3 tbsp maple syrup or agave
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Ice, lemon slices and mint to serve

## Instructions

1. Bring the water, turmeric, ginger, cinnamon and peppercorns to the boil. Reduce the heat and simmer for 10–15 minutes.
2. Remove from the heat, stir in the maple syrup and leave to cool.
3. Strain into a jug, add the lemon juice and chill.
4. Serve over ice with lemon slices and mint.

## Tips

- Fresh turmeric stains, so wear gloves and use a non-porous chopping board.
- Keeps in the fridge for up to 4 days.
