These cookies hold their shape when cut, bake up soft in the middle and are just waiting to be decorated. They're a favourite for holiday baking with kids.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup (170 g) vegan block butter, softened
- ¾ cup (150 g) caster sugar
- 3 tbsp plant milk
- 1½ tsp vanilla extract
- 2½ cups (310 g) plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp salt

**Icing**

- 2 cups (240 g) icing sugar
- 2–3 tbsp plant milk
- Food colouring and sprinkles

## Instructions

1. Beat the butter and sugar until fluffy. Add the milk and vanilla and mix well.
2. Add the flour, baking powder and salt and mix to a soft dough. Divide in two, flatten into discs, wrap and chill for 1 hour.
3. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Roll the dough to 5 mm thick on a floured surface and cut out shapes.
4. Bake on lined trays for 8–10 minutes, until set but not browned. Cool completely.
5. Mix the icing sugar with milk to a thick, smooth icing. Colour, decorate and let set.

## Tips

- Undecorated cookies freeze well for up to 3 months.
