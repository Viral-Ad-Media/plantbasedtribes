A cobbler is the most forgiving of desserts. Sliced peaches are tossed with sugar and spice, topped with dollops of tender biscuit dough flecked with crystallised ginger, and baked until bubbling. The ginger adds a gentle warmth that's perfect with the sweet fruit.

## Ingredients

**Filling**

- 1.2 kg ripe peaches, sliced (or 3 × 400 g tins, drained)
- ⅓ cup (65 g) brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon

**Ginger biscuits**

- 1½ cups (190 g) plain flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- ¼ tsp salt
- 5 tbsp cold vegan butter, cubed
- 2 tbsp crystallised ginger, finely chopped
- ⅔ cup (160 ml) cold plant milk

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F). Toss the peaches with the filling ingredients and spread in a 23 cm baking dish.
2. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, ground ginger and salt. Rub in the butter until it resembles breadcrumbs. Stir in the crystallised ginger.
3. Add the milk and stir just until a soft, sticky dough forms.
4. Drop spoonfuls of dough over the peaches, leaving gaps for steam to escape. Sprinkle with a little sugar.
5. Bake for 35–40 minutes until the biscuits are golden and the fruit is bubbling. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

## Tips

- Frozen peaches work too. Add them straight from frozen and bake for 10 minutes longer.
