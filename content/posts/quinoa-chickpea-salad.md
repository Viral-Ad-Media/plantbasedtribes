This is our go-to salad for packed lunches. Fluffy quinoa and chickpeas make it filling, cucumber and peppers add crunch, and the dressing is simply red pepper hummus thinned with lemon juice. It tastes even better the next day.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (180 g) quinoa, rinsed
- 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 150 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- ½ red onion, finely diced
- Large handful of parsley, chopped

**Dressing**

- ½ cup (120 g) roasted red pepper hummus
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2–3 tbsp water
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook the quinoa in 2 cups (480 ml) of water for 12–15 minutes until the water is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and let cool.
2. Whisk the hummus, lemon juice, olive oil and water together until pourable. Season.
3. Combine the quinoa, chickpeas, cucumber, pepper, tomatoes, onion and parsley in a large bowl.
4. Pour over the dressing and toss well.

## Tips

- Keeps for 4 days in the fridge, which makes it ideal for meal prep.
- Add crumbled vegan feta or toasted pumpkin seeds for extra texture.
