This is the banana bread we make every time the fruit bowl has a few too many spotty bananas. The riper the bananas, the sweeter and moister the loaf. No eggs needed: the bananas bind everything together.

## Ingredients

- 3 large very ripe bananas
- ⅓ cup (80 ml) neutral oil or melted coconut oil
- ½ cup (100 g) light brown sugar
- ¼ cup (60 ml) plant milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
- 2 cups (250 g) plain flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ cup (80 g) dark chocolate chips or chopped walnuts (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a 900 g (2 lb) loaf tin.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl. Whisk in the oil, sugar, milk, vanilla and vinegar.
3. Add the flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt and fold together until just combined. Fold in the chocolate or nuts.
4. Pour into the tin and bake for 50–60 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cover loosely with foil if it browns too quickly.
5. Cool in the tin for 15 minutes, then on a rack.

## Tips

- Freeze peeled overripe bananas so you're always ready to bake.
- Wrapped well, the loaf keeps for 4 days and freezes in slices.
