A baked sweet potato is a blank canvas, and this filling makes it a complete meal: warm lentils with cumin, cherry tomatoes and herbs. A generous drizzle of lemony tahini brings it all together.

## Ingredients

- 4 medium sweet potatoes
- 1 cup (200 g) green or brown lentils, rinsed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 red onion, finely diced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 150 g cherry tomatoes, quartered
- Handful each of parsley and mint, chopped
- Juice of ½ lemon

**Lemon tahini dressing**

- 3 tbsp tahini
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 3–4 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Prick the sweet potatoes, place on a tray and bake for 45–55 minutes until soft.
2. Meanwhile, simmer the lentils in plenty of water for 20–25 minutes until tender. Drain.
3. Heat the oil and soften the onion for 5 minutes. Add the cumin and cinnamon for 1 minute, then stir in the lentils, lemon juice and a good pinch of salt.
4. Take off the heat and fold in the tomatoes and herbs.
5. Whisk the dressing ingredients until smooth.
6. Split the sweet potatoes and spoon in the lentils. Drizzle with tahini dressing.

## Tips

- Use a tin of cooked lentils to save time.
