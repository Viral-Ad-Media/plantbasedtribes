There are few things more comforting than a bowl of tomato soup, and this one tastes like the height of summer even when you make it with tins. Slow-softened onion and garlic give it sweetness, a little tomato paste deepens the flavour, and a handful of fresh basil stirred in at the end makes it smell incredible.

Instead of cream, we blend in soaked cashews for a velvety finish that keeps the soup completely plant-based.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 × 400 g tins whole peeled tomatoes
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tsp sugar or maple syrup (optional, to balance acidity)
- ⅓ cup (45 g) raw cashews, soaked in hot water for 15 minutes
- A large handful of fresh basil, plus extra to serve
- Salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook for 8 minutes until soft and golden.
2. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes, stirring, until the paste darkens slightly.
3. Pour in the tomatoes, stock and sugar. Break the tomatoes up with a spoon, bring to a simmer and cook for 15 minutes.
4. Drain the cashews and add them to the pot along with the basil.
5. Blend until completely smooth, either with a stick blender or in batches in a jug blender (vent the lid so steam can escape).
6. Season generously with salt and pepper. Serve topped with torn basil and a drizzle of olive oil.

## Tips

- **Nut-free?** Swap the cashews for 3 tbsp of white beans or a splash of oat cream.
- Leftovers keep for 4 days in the fridge or 3 months in the freezer.
- Turn it into a meal with grilled cheese made from your favourite vegan cheese.
