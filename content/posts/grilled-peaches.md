Grilling brings out the natural sugars in ripe peaches, giving them caramelised stripes and a soft, jammy centre. Topped with cold whipped coconut cream and a drizzle of maple syrup, they make an effortless summer dessert.

## Ingredients

- 4 ripe but firm peaches, halved and stoned
- 1 tbsp coconut oil, melted
- 2 tbsp maple syrup, plus extra to serve
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 × 400 ml tin full-fat coconut milk, chilled overnight
- 2 tbsp toasted flaked almonds
- Fresh mint, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat a grill or griddle pan to medium-high.
2. Brush the cut sides of the peaches with coconut oil, maple syrup and cinnamon.
3. Grill cut-side down for 4–5 minutes until caramelised, then turn and cook for 2 minutes more.
4. Scoop the solid cream from the top of the chilled coconut milk and whip until fluffy.
5. Serve the warm peaches with a spoonful of coconut cream, a drizzle of maple, almonds and mint.

## Tips

- Nectarines and plums work just as well.
- Swap the coconut cream for a scoop of vegan vanilla ice cream.
