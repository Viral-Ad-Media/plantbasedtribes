Young green jackfruit has a mild flavour and a stringy texture that shreds just like pulled meat. Simmered in a sticky, smoky barbecue sauce, it makes a sandwich that wins over even the most committed carnivores.

## Ingredients

- 2 × 400 g tins young green jackfruit in water or brine, drained and rinsed
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 cup (250 ml) vegan barbecue sauce
- ½ cup (120 ml) water
- 4 burger buns

**Quick slaw**

- 2 cups shredded red and white cabbage
- 1 carrot, grated
- 2 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Cut away the hard core from each piece of jackfruit and squeeze out the seeds.
2. Heat the oil in a pan. Cook the onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic, paprika and cumin.
3. Add the jackfruit, barbecue sauce and water. Simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Shred the jackfruit with two forks and cook for 5 minutes more until sticky.
5. Toss the slaw ingredients together.
6. Pile the jackfruit into toasted buns and top with slaw.

## Tips

- Make sure you buy young green jackfruit in water or brine, not ripe jackfruit in syrup.
- For extra char, spread the sauced jackfruit on a tray and grill for 5 minutes.
