The star of this salad is the walnut taco "meat": walnuts pulsed with taco spices into a crumbly, savoury topping that needs no cooking. Pile it onto crisp lettuce with beans, corn, tomatoes and a creamy lime dressing.

## Ingredients

**Walnut taco meat**

- 1½ cups (150 g) walnuts
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp tamari

**Salad**

- 1 large romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1 × 400 g tin black beans, drained
- 1 cup (150 g) sweetcorn
- 200 g cherry tomatoes, quartered
- 1 avocado, diced
- Crushed tortilla chips

**Lime dressing**

- ½ cup plain plant yoghurt
- Juice of 1 lime
- Small handful of coriander
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Pulse the walnuts, spices and tamari in a food processor until crumbly. Don't over-process.
2. Blend or whisk the dressing ingredients.
3. Divide the lettuce between bowls. Top with the beans, corn, tomatoes, avocado and walnut meat.
4. Drizzle with dressing and finish with crushed tortilla chips.

## Tips

- Warm the walnut meat in a dry pan for 3 minutes for extra toasty flavour.
- Not nut-friendly? Use lentils cooked with the same spices.
