Split peas are one of the cheapest ingredients in the shop and one of the most satisfying. Simmered slowly with onion, carrot, a little smoked paprika and cumin, they break down into a thick, creamy soup without any blending.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 cups (400 g) yellow split peas, rinsed
- 2 litres vegetable stock
- 1 bay leaf
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Cook the onion, carrot and celery for 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and stir for 1 minute.
3. Add the split peas, stock and bay leaf. Bring to the boil, skim any foam, then simmer partly covered for 50–60 minutes, stirring now and then, until the peas collapse.
4. Remove the bay leaf, add the lemon juice and season.
5. Serve as is, or blend for a smoother texture.

## Tips

- The soup thickens a lot as it cools. Add water when reheating.
- Top with crispy fried onions or a drizzle of chilli oil.
