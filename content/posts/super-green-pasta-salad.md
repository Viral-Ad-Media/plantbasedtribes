This pasta salad is as green as it gets: a nut-free spinach and basil pesto coats the pasta, and peas, broccoli and courgette add sweetness and crunch. It's brilliant for picnics, barbecues and lunchboxes.

## Ingredients

- 350 g short pasta, such as fusilli
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1 cup (150 g) frozen peas
- 1 courgette (zucchini), diced
- Handful of rocket (arugula)

**Green pesto**

- 2 big handfuls of spinach
- 1 large bunch of basil
- 3 tbsp pumpkin or sunflower seeds
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 garlic clove
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 5 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta in salted water. Add the broccoli for the last 3 minutes and the peas for the last minute. Drain and rinse under cold water.
2. Blend all the pesto ingredients until smooth, adding a splash of water if needed.
3. Toss the pasta and vegetables with the pesto and raw courgette.
4. Fold through the rocket just before serving.

## Tips

- Use gluten-free pasta if needed; the rest of the recipe is gluten-free.
- Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Add a squeeze of lemon to refresh it.
