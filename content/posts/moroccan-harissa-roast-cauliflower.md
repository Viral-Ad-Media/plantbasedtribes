Roasting a whole cauliflower makes a dramatic centrepiece for any table. Coated in a smoky, spicy harissa paste, it roasts until tender inside and crisp at the edges. Serve with fluffy couscous and cooling plant yoghurt.

## Ingredients

- 1 large cauliflower
- 3 tbsp harissa paste
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 cup (180 g) couscous
- Handful each of mint and parsley
- Seeds from ½ pomegranate
- Plain plant yoghurt, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Trim the cauliflower leaves and cut the stem so it sits flat.
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the whole cauliflower for 5 minutes. Drain well.
3. Mix the harissa, oil, maple syrup, cumin and lemon juice. Brush all over the cauliflower.
4. Roast in a baking dish for 45–55 minutes until a knife slides easily into the centre.
5. Meanwhile, pour boiling water over the couscous, cover for 5 minutes, then fluff and stir through the herbs.
6. Serve the cauliflower in wedges on the couscous with pomegranate seeds and a spoonful of yoghurt.

## Tips

- Use quinoa instead of couscous to make it gluten-free.
