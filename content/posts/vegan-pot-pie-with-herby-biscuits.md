This pot pie skips the fiddly pastry in favour of easy drop biscuits flecked with fresh herbs. Underneath is a creamy, savoury stew of vegetables and white beans that's comforting and filling.

## Ingredients

**Filling**

- 3 tbsp vegan butter or olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ⅓ cup (40 g) plain flour
- 750 ml vegetable stock
- 250 ml unsweetened plant milk
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 × 400 g tin white beans, drained
- 1 cup (150 g) frozen peas
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- Salt and pepper

**Herby biscuits**

- 2 cups (250 g) plain flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh herbs (parsley, chives, thyme)
- 6 tbsp cold vegan butter, cubed
- ¾ cup (180 ml) cold plant milk

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Melt the butter in a large ovenproof pan. Cook the onion, carrot and celery for 8 minutes. Add the mushrooms and garlic and cook for 5 minutes more.
3. Stir in the flour for 1 minute, then slowly whisk in the stock and milk. Add the thyme and simmer, stirring, until thickened.
4. Stir in the beans, peas and nutritional yeast. Season well.
5. For the biscuits, mix the flour, baking powder, salt and herbs. Rub in the butter, then stir in the milk to make a soft dough.
6. Drop spoonfuls of dough over the filling. Bake for 25 minutes until the biscuits are golden.

## Tips

- If your pan isn't ovenproof, transfer the filling to a baking dish before adding the biscuits.
