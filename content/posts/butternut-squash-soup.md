Roasting the squash before it goes into the pot is the secret to this soup. The edges caramelise in the oven and give the finished bowl a deep, nutty sweetness that boiling alone can't match. A little cumin and smoked paprika keep things interesting, and coconut milk makes it rich and creamy.

## Ingredients

- 1 large butternut squash (about 1.2 kg), peeled, seeded and cubed
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 1 red onion, quartered
- 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 × 400 ml tin coconut milk (reserve 2 tbsp to serve)
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt and pepper
- Toasted pumpkin seeds, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Toss the squash, carrot, onion and garlic with the oil, cumin, paprika and a good pinch of salt on a large tray.
2. Roast for 35–40 minutes, turning once, until soft and caramelised at the edges.
3. Squeeze the garlic out of its skins and tip everything into a large pot. Add the stock and coconut milk and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth. Add more stock if you like a thinner soup.
5. Stir in the lemon juice and season to taste.
6. Serve with a swirl of coconut milk and a scattering of toasted pumpkin seeds.

## Tips

- Short on time? Skip roasting and simmer the vegetables in the stock for 25 minutes instead.
- A pinch of chilli flakes or a teaspoon of curry paste turns this into a spicier soup.
- Freezes beautifully for up to 3 months.
