Berries make any green smoothie taste like a treat. This one gets its sweetness from banana and mixed berries, a hidden handful of spinach and a spoonful of ground flaxseed for fibre and omega-3s.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups (225 g) frozen mixed berries
- 1 banana
- 2 handfuls of spinach
- 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
- 1 tbsp almond butter
- 1 cup (240 ml) plant milk
- ½ cup plain plant yoghurt (optional, for extra creaminess)

## Instructions

1. Add the milk and spinach to the blender and blend until smooth.
2. Add the remaining ingredients and blend until thick and creamy.
3. Serve straight away, or pour into a bowl and top with granola and fresh fruit.

## Tips

- Use a frozen banana for an extra-thick, ice cream-like texture.
