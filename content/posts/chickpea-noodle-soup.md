This is the soup we make whenever someone's feeling under the weather. Chickpeas stand in for chicken, and a spoonful of nutritional yeast and a little soy sauce give the broth that savoury, golden depth. Kids love it as much as grown-ups.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery sticks, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1.5 litres vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 100 g egg-free noodles or small pasta
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Cook the onion, carrot and celery for 7 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and thyme for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the stock, soy sauce and nutritional yeast. Add the chickpeas and bring to a simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Add the noodles and cook according to the packet, usually 6–8 minutes.
5. Stir in the lemon juice and parsley. Season and serve.

## Tips

- Check your noodles are egg-free; many dried noodles contain egg.
- For gluten-free, use rice noodles and tamari instead of soy sauce.
