This lasagna is a labour of love that's well worth it. Garlicky mushrooms and spinach are layered with a herby tomato sauce and a silky cashew béchamel, then baked until bubbling and golden.

## Ingredients

**Tomato sauce**

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 × 400 g tins chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Handful of basil

**Mushroom and spinach layer**

- 500 g mushrooms, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 300 g spinach

**Cashew béchamel**

- 1 cup (140 g) cashews, soaked for 30 minutes
- 2 cups (480 ml) unsweetened plant milk
- 3 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Pinch of nutmeg and salt

**To assemble**

- 12 egg-free lasagne sheets

## Instructions

1. For the sauce, cook the onion in oil for 6 minutes, add the garlic, then the tomatoes, paste and oregano. Simmer for 15 minutes and stir in the basil.
2. Fry the mushrooms over high heat until golden, add the garlic, then wilt in the spinach. Season and drain off any liquid.
3. Blend all the béchamel ingredients until completely smooth.
4. Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F). In a large baking dish, layer a third of the tomato sauce, then lasagne sheets, half the mushroom mixture and a third of the béchamel. Repeat, then finish with sheets, the remaining tomato sauce and béchamel.
5. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes. Uncover and bake for 15 minutes more until golden.
6. Rest for 15 minutes before slicing.

## Tips

- Make it a day ahead; lasagna slices more neatly once it has rested.
