These meatballs are made from lentils, mushrooms and oats, so they're full of savoury flavour and hold together well. A sticky Asian-style glaze turns them into something special, and they're delicious tossed through noodles with greens.

## Ingredients

**Meatballs**

- 1 × 400 g tin green lentils, drained
- 150 g mushrooms, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- ¾ cup (70 g) rolled oats
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tbsp water
- 2 spring onions, finely chopped

**Glaze**

- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 3 tbsp water

**To serve**

- 250 g noodles
- 2 pak choi or a few handfuls of greens
- Sesame seeds and sliced spring onions

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Fry the mushrooms and garlic in the oil for 5–6 minutes until the liquid has evaporated.
2. Pulse the oats in a food processor, then add the lentils, mushrooms and remaining meatball ingredients. Pulse until combined but still with some texture.
3. Roll into 20 balls, place on a lined tray and bake for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway.
4. Simmer the glaze ingredients in a large pan until thick and glossy. Add the meatballs and toss to coat.
5. Cook the noodles and stir-fry the greens. Serve the meatballs over the noodles with sesame seeds and spring onions.

## Tips

- The meatballs freeze well before or after baking.
