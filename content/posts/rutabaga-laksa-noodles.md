Rutabaga (also called swede) makes surprisingly good noodles. Spiralised and simmered briefly, it turns tender with a gentle sweetness that suits a fragrant, spicy laksa broth.

## Ingredients

- 1 large rutabaga (swede), peeled and spiralised
- 1 tbsp oil
- 3 tbsp vegan laksa paste (or red curry paste with 1 tsp turmeric)
- 1 lemongrass stalk, bruised
- 1 × 400 ml tin coconut milk
- 750 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp tamari
- 1 tsp sugar
- 200 g tofu puffs or firm tofu, cubed
- 2 handfuls of bean sprouts
- Juice of 1 lime
- Coriander, mint, sliced chilli and lime wedges, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Fry the laksa paste and lemongrass for 2 minutes until fragrant.
2. Add the coconut milk, stock, tamari and sugar. Simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Add the tofu and rutabaga noodles and simmer for 4–5 minutes until the noodles are just tender.
4. Remove the lemongrass and stir in the lime juice.
5. Ladle into bowls and top with bean sprouts, herbs and chilli.

## Tips

- No spiraliser? Use a julienne peeler or swap in rice noodles.
- Check that your laksa paste is shrimp-free.
