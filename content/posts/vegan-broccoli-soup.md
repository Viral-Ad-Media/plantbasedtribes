If you miss broccoli-cheddar soup, this one's for you. A potato blended in with the broccoli makes it thick and creamy, and nutritional yeast adds a savoury, cheesy note without any dairy. Cooking the broccoli briefly keeps the colour vivid.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 medium potato, peeled and diced
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 2 heads broccoli (about 600 g), cut into florets, stalks peeled and sliced
- 3 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a pot and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
2. Add the potato and stock and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Add the broccoli and simmer for 5–6 minutes, just until tender and still bright green.
4. Add the nutritional yeast, mustard and lemon juice and blend until smooth.
5. Season and serve with toasted seeds or croutons.

## Tips

- Reserve a few small florets, roast them and use them as a topping.
- Most stock cubes contain plenty of salt, so taste before adding more.
