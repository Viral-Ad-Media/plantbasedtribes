"But where do you get your protein?" If you eat plant-based, you'll hear this a lot. The short answer: from lots of places. Here's a practical guide to the best plant-based protein sources and how to include them every day.

## How much protein do you need?

General guidelines for healthy adults suggest around 0.8 g of protein per kilogram of body weight per day. That's about 56 g for a 70 kg adult. Needs are higher for very active people, older adults and during pregnancy. Most people eating a varied plant-based diet with enough calories meet their needs comfortably.

## Top plant protein sources

The figures below are approximate and vary by brand and preparation.

| Food | Serving | Protein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seitan | 100 g | ~25 g |
| Tempeh | 100 g | ~19 g |
| Firm tofu | 100 g | ~12–17 g |
| Edamame, shelled | 1 cup (155 g) | ~18 g |
| Lentils, cooked | 1 cup (200 g) | ~18 g |
| Chickpeas, cooked | 1 cup (165 g) | ~14 g |
| Black beans, cooked | 1 cup (170 g) | ~15 g |
| Hemp seeds | 3 tbsp (30 g) | ~10 g |
| Peanut butter | 2 tbsp (32 g) | ~7 g |
| Quinoa, cooked | 1 cup (185 g) | ~8 g |
| Rolled oats | ½ cup (40 g) | ~5 g |
| Soya milk | 1 cup (250 ml) | ~7–8 g |

## What about "complete" protein?

Proteins are made of amino acids, nine of which we must get from food. Soya, quinoa and hemp contain all nine in good amounts. Other plant foods may be lower in one or two, but you don't need to combine them at every meal. Eating a variety of protein sources across the day covers your needs.

## Easy ways to add protein to every meal

- **Breakfast:** Stir hemp seeds or peanut butter into oats, make a tofu scramble or blend soya milk into smoothies.
- **Lunch:** Add chickpeas or lentils to salads, choose hummus in wraps or top soups with toasted seeds.
- **Dinner:** Build meals around beans, lentils, tofu or tempeh. Try our [Vegan Lentil Burgers](/vegan-lentil-burgers) or [Marinated Tofu Salad](/marinated-tofu-salad).
- **Snacks:** Edamame, roasted chickpeas, nuts or a [protein-packed snack bar](/almond-cherry-pepita-bars).

## The bottom line

You don't need meat to get enough protein. Include a protein-rich plant food at every meal, eat a variety across the day and make sure you're eating enough overall. If you have specific health needs, a registered dietitian can help you plan.
