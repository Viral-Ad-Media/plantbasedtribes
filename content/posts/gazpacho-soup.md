When it's too hot to cook, gazpacho is the answer. Ripe tomatoes, cucumber, pepper and a little garlic are blended with olive oil and sherry vinegar into a cool, refreshing soup. It's only as good as your tomatoes, so make it in high summer when they're at their best.

## Ingredients

- 1 kg very ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 1 cucumber, peeled and chopped
- 1 green or red pepper, seeded and chopped
- 1 small red onion or 2 spring onions, chopped
- 1 garlic clove
- 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp sherry vinegar
- 1 tsp salt
- Cold water, as needed

## Instructions

1. Put the tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, onion and garlic in a blender with the salt. Blend until very smooth.
2. With the motor running, drizzle in the olive oil and vinegar.
3. Taste and adjust with more salt or vinegar. Add a little cold water if it's too thick.
4. Strain through a sieve for a silky texture, or leave as is.
5. Chill for at least 1 hour. Serve with finely diced cucumber, pepper and a drizzle of oil.

## Tips

- Traditional gazpacho is thickened with day-old bread; leave it out to keep this gluten-free.
- It keeps for 2 days in the fridge and tastes even better on day two.
