This fusion dish takes a classic peanut noodle sauce and adds warming Indian spices: garam masala, cumin and fresh ginger. The result is rich, fragrant and a little spicy, with plenty of crunch from fresh vegetables.

## Ingredients

- 250 g noodles (egg-free wheat, rice or wholewheat spaghetti)
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 red pepper, thinly sliced
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- Handful of coriander and chopped roasted peanuts

**Spiced peanut sauce**

- 4 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- 4–5 tbsp hot water

## Instructions

1. Cook the noodles according to the packet. Drain, reserving a little of the cooking water.
2. Whisk the sauce ingredients together until smooth.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan or wok. Stir-fry the pepper, cabbage and carrot for 2–3 minutes until just tender.
4. Add the noodles and sauce and toss together, loosening with a splash of noodle water if needed.
5. Serve topped with spring onions, coriander and peanuts.

## Tips

- Add tofu or chickpeas for extra protein.
