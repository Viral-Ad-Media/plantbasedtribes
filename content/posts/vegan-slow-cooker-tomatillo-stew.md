Tomatillos give this stew a bright, tangy flavour that's quite different from tomato-based stews. Everything goes into the slow cooker in the morning, and by dinner you'll have a fragrant pot ready for warm tortillas.

## Ingredients

- 500 g tomatillos, husked and chopped (or a 500 g jar of salsa verde)
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 poblano peppers, seeded and diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 medium potatoes, cubed
- 2 × 400 g tins white beans, drained
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 750 ml vegetable stock
- Juice of 1 lime
- Large handful of coriander, chopped
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Add everything except the lime and coriander to the slow cooker.
2. Cook on Low for 6–7 hours or High for 3–4 hours, until the potatoes are tender.
3. Mash a few of the beans and potatoes against the side of the pot to thicken the stew.
4. Stir in the lime juice and coriander. Season with salt.
5. Serve with warm corn tortillas, avocado and a spoonful of plant yoghurt.

## Tips

- No poblanos? Use green peppers and add a pinch more chilli.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months.
