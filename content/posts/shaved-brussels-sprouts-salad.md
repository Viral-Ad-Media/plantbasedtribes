Raw Brussels sprouts, sliced paper-thin, are crisp, fresh and slightly sweet, nothing like the soggy sprouts of childhood dinners. Tossed with toasted pecans, crisp apple and a punchy maple-mustard dressing, they make a salad that holds up beautifully and is perfect for festive tables.

## Ingredients

- 400 g Brussels sprouts, trimmed
- 1 crisp apple, thinly sliced
- ½ cup (60 g) pecans
- ⅓ cup (40 g) dried cranberries
- 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds

**Dressing**

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Toast the pecans in a dry pan over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until fragrant. Roughly chop.
2. Thinly slice the sprouts with a sharp knife, a mandoline or the slicing disc of a food processor.
3. Whisk the dressing ingredients together in a large bowl.
4. Add the sprouts and toss well, massaging lightly so the dressing coats every shred.
5. Add the apple, cranberries, pecans and pumpkin seeds. Toss again and serve.

## Tips

- This salad can be dressed up to 2 hours ahead; it softens slightly but stays crunchy.
- Swap pecans for walnuts or hazelnuts.
