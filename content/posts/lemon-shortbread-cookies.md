Classic shortbread is mostly butter, so the key to a great vegan version is a good block-style plant butter. These cookies are tender and crumbly with a fresh lemon flavour, and the glaze adds a sweet-sharp finish.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (225 g) vegan block butter, softened
- ½ cup (100 g) caster sugar
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups (250 g) plain flour
- ¼ cup (30 g) cornflour (cornstarch)
- ¼ tsp salt

**Glaze**

- 1 cup (120 g) icing sugar
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Beat the butter, sugar and lemon zest until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Mix in the vanilla.
2. Add the flour, cornflour and salt and mix just until a soft dough forms.
3. Shape the dough into a log about 5 cm across, wrap and chill for 30 minutes.
4. Heat the oven to 170°C (340°F). Slice the log into 1 cm rounds and place on a lined tray.
5. Bake for 13–15 minutes until just pale golden at the edges. Cool completely.
6. Whisk the icing sugar and lemon juice into a thick glaze and drizzle over the cookies.

## Tips

- Use a firm block butter rather than a soft spread, or the cookies will spread too much.
- Store in an airtight tin for up to a week.
