Kale and noodles might not be an obvious pairing, but stir-fried until just wilted and coated in creamy peanut sauce, kale is fantastic. This bowl comes together in 20 minutes from mostly pantry ingredients.

## Ingredients

- 150 g noodles of your choice
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 bunch kale, stems removed, leaves torn
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 tbsp roasted peanuts, chopped

**Peanut-lime sauce**

- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp sriracha
- 3 tbsp hot water

## Instructions

1. Cook the noodles according to the packet. Drain.
2. Whisk the sauce ingredients until smooth.
3. Heat the oil in a wok. Stir-fry the kale, carrot and pepper for 3 minutes until the kale has wilted.
4. Add the noodles and sauce and toss together until coated.
5. Serve in bowls topped with chopped peanuts.

## Tips

- Use rice noodles and tamari for a gluten-free bowl.
