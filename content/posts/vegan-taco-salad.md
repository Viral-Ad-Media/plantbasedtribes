This taco salad uses lentils simmered with smoky spices as a filling, budget-friendly base. Paired with crisp veg and a creamy chipotle crema, it's a weeknight dinner the whole family will enjoy.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp taco seasoning (or 1 tsp each cumin, paprika and chilli powder)
- 1 cup (200 g) brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 head romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 1 cup (150 g) sweetcorn
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Pickled red onions and coriander

**Chipotle crema**

- ½ cup (70 g) cashews, soaked for 15 minutes
- 1 chipotle in adobo (or ½ tsp smoked paprika)
- Juice of 1 lime
- ⅓ cup water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil and cook the onion for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and spices for 1 minute.
2. Add the lentils, stock and tomato paste. Simmer for 20 minutes until tender and most of the liquid has gone.
3. Blend the crema ingredients until smooth.
4. Fill bowls with lettuce, pepper, corn and avocado. Top with the warm lentils, crema, pickled onions and coriander.

## Tips

- Make a double batch of the lentils. They're great in tacos and burritos too.
