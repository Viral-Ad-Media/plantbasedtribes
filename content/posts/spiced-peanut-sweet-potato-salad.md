Sweet potato and peanut are a match made in heaven. The potatoes are roasted with cumin and smoked paprika until caramelised, then tossed with leafy greens, red onion and coriander and drizzled with a creamy peanut-lime dressing.

## Ingredients

- 2 large sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 × 400 g tin chickpeas, drained
- 4 handfuls of mixed salad leaves or spinach
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- Handful of coriander
- 2 tbsp roasted peanuts, chopped

**Peanut dressing**

- 3 tbsp smooth peanut butter
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp tamari or soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- ½ tsp chilli flakes
- 3–4 tbsp warm water

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Toss the sweet potato and chickpeas with oil, cumin, paprika and salt. Roast for 25–30 minutes, turning once.
2. Whisk the dressing ingredients until smooth, adding water until pourable.
3. Arrange the leaves on a platter. Top with the warm sweet potato and chickpeas and the red onion.
4. Drizzle with the dressing and scatter with coriander and chopped peanuts.

## Tips

- For a nut-free version, use sunflower seed butter.
- Leftovers make a great packed lunch; keep the dressing separate.
