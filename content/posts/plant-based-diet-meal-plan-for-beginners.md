Not sure what to cook during your first week of plant-based eating? This meal plan takes the guesswork out. It uses recipes from the site, repeats some ingredients to keep shopping simple and makes clever use of leftovers.

Adjust portions to your appetite and swap meals around freely.

## Day 1

- **Breakfast:** Overnight oats with plant milk, chia seeds and berries
- **Lunch:** [Quinoa Chickpea Salad](/quinoa-chickpea-salad) (make a double batch for tomorrow)
- **Dinner:** [Hearty Red Lentil Soup](/lentil-soup) with crusty bread

## Day 2

- **Breakfast:** [Green Berry Smoothie](/green-berry-smoothie)
- **Lunch:** Leftover quinoa chickpea salad
- **Dinner:** [Peanut Noodle Kale Bowl](/peanut-noodles-kale-bowl)

## Day 3

- **Breakfast:** Wholegrain toast with avocado, tomatoes and pumpkin seeds
- **Lunch:** Leftover lentil soup
- **Dinner:** [Vegan Taco Salad with Smoky Lentils](/vegan-taco-salad)

## Day 4

- **Breakfast:** Overnight oats with banana and peanut butter
- **Lunch:** [Kale Salad with Carrot Ginger Dressing](/kale-salad-with-carrot-ginger-dressing-recipe)
- **Dinner:** [Thai Red Curry Noodle Soup](/asian-red-curry-soup)

## Day 5

- **Breakfast:** Tofu scramble with spinach and toast
- **Lunch:** Leftover curry soup
- **Dinner:** [Vegan Lentil Burgers](/vegan-lentil-burgers) with sweet potato wedges

## Day 6

- **Breakfast:** [Vegan Blueberry Muffins](/vegan-blueberry-muffins) with fruit and plant yoghurt
- **Lunch:** Leftover lentil burger in a wrap with salad
- **Dinner:** [Vegan Spinach and Mushroom Lasagna](/spinach-and-mushroom-lasagna)

## Day 7

- **Breakfast:** [Pumpkin Pie Smoothie](/pumpkin-pie-smoothie-drink-recipe)
- **Lunch:** Leftover lasagna with a green salad
- **Dinner:** [Roasted Butternut Squash Soup](/butternut-squash-soup)
- **Treat:** [Cinnamon Baked Apples](/baked-apples)

## Snack ideas

- Fruit with a handful of nuts
- Hummus with carrot and cucumber sticks
- [Almond Cherry Pepita Bars](/almond-cherry-pepita-bars)
- Roasted chickpeas

## Shopping list basics

**Pantry:** red and green lentils, tinned chickpeas and black beans, quinoa, oats, noodles, lasagne sheets, peanut butter, tahini, soy sauce, coconut milk, chopped tomatoes, vegetable stock, spices (cumin, smoked paprika, turmeric), chia and flax seeds.

**Fresh:** onions, garlic, ginger, carrots, kale, spinach, sweet potatoes, butternut squash, mushrooms, peppers, cucumbers, tomatoes, avocados, bananas, berries, lemons and limes, fresh herbs.

**Chilled:** plant milk, plant yoghurt, firm tofu.

## Tips for success

- **Prep on Sunday.** Cook a pot of grains, wash and chop vegetables and make one dressing.
- **Cook once, eat twice.** Most dinners in this plan make enough for lunch the next day.
- **Remember B12.** If you're eating fully plant-based, take a B12 supplement.
