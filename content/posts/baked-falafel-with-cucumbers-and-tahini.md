Authentic falafel is made with soaked raw chickpeas rather than tinned, which gives it a light, fluffy texture inside. Baking instead of frying keeps them simple to make at home and still gives a lovely golden crust.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200 g) dried chickpeas, soaked in cold water for 12–24 hours
- 1 small onion, roughly chopped
- 3 garlic cloves
- Large handful each of parsley and coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp olive oil

**To serve**

- 2 cucumbers, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- Lemon tahini sauce (3 tbsp tahini, juice of 1 lemon, 1 grated garlic clove and water to thin)
- Pitta or flatbreads, pickled red onion

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and brush a tray with 2 tbsp of the oil.
2. Drain the chickpeas well. Pulse with the onion, garlic, herbs, spices, baking powder and salt in a food processor until finely chopped and holding together, like coarse sand.
3. Shape into 20 small patties and place on the tray. Brush with the remaining oil.
4. Bake for 25 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp.
5. Whisk the tahini sauce until smooth. Serve the falafel with cucumber, tomato, tahini and warm bread.

## Tips

- Don't use tinned chickpeas. They're too soft and the falafel will fall apart.
- Use gluten-free flatbreads to keep the meal gluten-free.
