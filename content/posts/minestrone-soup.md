Minestrone is less a fixed recipe and more a way of cooking: start with a good soffritto of onion, carrot and celery, add whatever vegetables are in season, then bulk it out with beans and small pasta. This version is the one we come back to again and again.

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery sticks, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 courgette (zucchini), diced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 × 400 g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1.5 litres vegetable stock
- 1 × 400 g tin cannellini or borlotti beans, drained
- ¾ cup (80 g) small pasta, such as ditalini
- 2 handfuls of chopped kale or cabbage
- Salt, pepper and fresh basil to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot. Add the onion, carrot and celery with a pinch of salt and cook gently for 10 minutes until soft.
2. Add the garlic, courgette, oregano and bay leaf and cook for 3 minutes.
3. Pour in the tomatoes and stock. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes.
4. Add the beans and pasta and simmer for another 8–10 minutes, until the pasta is tender.
5. Stir in the kale for the final 2 minutes. Remove the bay leaf and season well.
6. Serve with torn basil and a drizzle of good olive oil.

## Tips

- For gluten-free minestrone, use rice or gluten-free pasta.
- If you plan to freeze it, cook the pasta separately and add it when reheating so it doesn't go soft.
